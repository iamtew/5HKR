# Effects

The Effects page is a send from the lanes, not a secret wet mix you forgot to ask for. Click a name in the left rail to select it; **On** adds it to the chain. Drag the grip to reorder. The right rail is the same lane **FX** sends as Main (dry/wet into the chain). If those sends are at zero, the chain is silent and you will wonder why the pretty knobs do nothing.

Chorus, Delay, Flanger, Phaser, Reverb, Compressor, Distortion, Equalizer, Filter.

Delay, because people always ask: Sync, Div or Time, Fb, Cut, Ping, HP, Spread.

The insert Equalizer on this page lives on the send. It is not the post-EQ page. Two equalizers, two jobs, one less argument.
