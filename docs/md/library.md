# Library

**Init Patch** / **Init Bank** live in the title bar. Chevrons step the list; click for Init / Save / Save As / Export / Import.

Patches (`.5hkrp`) are knobs. Banks (`.5hkrb`) are patterns. They live under `%AppData%\Stupid Audio\5HKR\Patches` and `...\Banks`. That split is on purpose: you can keep a groove and swap the sound, or keep the sound and bring a different groove to the party.
