5HKR

Shaker. Percussion overlay.

Hold a MIDI key and 5HKR sits on top of whatever you are playing: eight analog-ish lanes on a sixteenth grid, banks you can key-lock, per-lane swing and FX send, a mixer, delay / reverb / chorus / flanger / phaser, post EQ, and a sidechain duck. Drop a one-shot on a lane if you want, but this is not a sample library.

It is part of Stupid Audio — five free instruments in one pack. The other four live over there.

5HKR-DEMO1.mp3

GitHub

VST3 and standalone. Hold C1 (Ableton C0) for bank 1 pattern 1, same trick as TEW03.

Main

Eight lanes: hats, crash, cymbal, shaker, clave, cowbell, rim. Pick 707, 808, 909, or Linn per lane, or drop a wav / aiff / flac / ogg and that lane becomes Sample. Click a cell through off, ghost, on (cyan), accent (magenta). Shift-click for flam or ratchet. Right-click pins the loop end.

Each lane gets tune, swing, FX send, and probability. Banks and patterns live here, plus copy / paste / fill / random / clear. Hold Fill (or MIDI C3) for the bank's fill without leaving the pattern you picked.

Mixer

One strip per lane: trim, a little 3-band EQ, a DJ filter (left high-pass, centre off, right low-pass), pan, mute, solo, and a fader with a thin meter behind it. Mute and solo match Main, so you will not argue with yourself.

Effects

Click a name in the left rail, hit On, and it joins the send. Drag the grip to reorder. Chorus, delay, flanger, phaser, reverb, compressor, distortion, equalizer, filter. Turn a lane's FX send up or the chain has nothing to chew on.

EQ

Five bands after the effects. Toggle it, drag nodes for frequency and gain, scroll for Q. Inspector on the left if you like numbers more than dragging.

Sidechain

Ducks the lanes from the plugin audio input. Off until you turn it on, so it will not surprise you. HP / LP pick what the ducker listens to; Listen puts that key on the output so you can hear it. Then thresh, amount, attack, release, mix. The plot under the knobs is that filtered key.

Title-bar chevrons flip pages. Click 5HKR in the corner for About.

Download 5HKR

The Windows installer is the easy path. It can put the VST3, the standalone app, or both on the machine, and it adds a Start menu shortcut for the app.

Windows installer

The VST3 zip is just the plugin. Drop it where your DAW looks for VST3s. Mac builds are ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper will sulk until you right-click Open the first time.

Windows Linux macOS

The standalone zip is the app, no DAW required. Same Gatekeeper story on Mac. Linux wants the usual system ALSA, FreeType, fontconfig, and X11 libraries.
